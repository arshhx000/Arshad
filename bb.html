Questions

Everything you need to know.

Still unsure? Send a brief — it's obligation-free and we'll point you in the right direction.

What will I receive at the end?+

Your approved primary logo, supporting versions, colour values, and export files for web and print — SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPG.

How long does a project take?+

First directions usually arrive within three business days of a complete brief. Final timelines depend on feedback speed.

Can I request revisions?+

Yes — your chosen direction is refined collaboratively, focused on your goals so every round moves forward.

Will I own the final logo?+

Once the project is paid and complete, the approved identity is yours. Font or asset licences are discussed upfront.

What does a project cost?+

Quoted by scope and application count. Start with a brief and we'll send a clear recommendation and price.
